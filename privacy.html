Windless Castle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Windless Castle is a single-player, offline role-playing game for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Short version: it collects none.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Data on your device

The only thing the app remembers is your music and sound-effects on/off settings, stored locally on your device. It never leaves your device.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page and the app's App Store listing will be updated before a release that changes it.
